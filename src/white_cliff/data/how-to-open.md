This tool explains the numbers in a public company filing. It does not recommend buying, selling or holding any security, and it is not investment advice.

# How to open White Cliff

There are three files. Pick the one that matches the computer.

- A Mac with an Apple chip, such as M1, M2, M3, or M4: WhiteCliff-Mac-Apple-silicon.zip
- An older Mac with an Intel chip: WhiteCliff-Mac-Intel.zip
- A Windows computer: WhiteCliff-Windows.zip

Unzip the file. On a Mac, double-click White Cliff. On Windows, double-click White Cliff.exe. There is nothing else to install.

The first time, a page asks for your name and your email. After that, type a company name or ticker and press Explain.

The first lookup is the first live one; the app was tested with saved filings.

The file is not signed. The computer does not know who published it, so the computer shows a warning before it opens. That warning is expected. The steps below say which button to press. Do not move the program to the trash.

## Mac

Unzip the file. Move White Cliff into Applications if you like. Double-click White Cliff.

The Mac shows a message that it cannot check the program, or that the program was not opened. Click Done. Do not click Move to Trash.

Then click the Apple menu at the top left of the screen. Click System Settings. Click Privacy & Security. Scroll down to the Security heading. Click Open Anyway. Type the computer password, or use Touch ID, when the Mac asks. If a second message appears, click Open.

The Open Anyway button stays for about an hour after the double-click. If the button is not there, double-click White Cliff again, then go back to Privacy & Security.

After that, double-clicking White Cliff opens it. The browser shows a private page on this computer. Opening White Cliff again opens the page that is already running. The page closes after 30 minutes with no use.

## Windows

Unzip the file. Double-click White Cliff.exe.

Windows shows a blue window. The title is "Windows protected your PC". Click More info. The window then names the program and says the publisher is unknown. Click Run anyway.

The browser shows a private page on this computer. Opening White Cliff again opens the page that is already running. The page closes after 30 minutes with no use.

If the blue window says the program was blocked and there is no Run anyway button, the computer has a stricter check turned on. The steps above are for the usual blue window.

## What you see in the page

Large type. The disclaimer is at the top. The first time, the page asks for your name and your email and says why. After you save, there is one box, "Company name or ticker", and an Explain button.
